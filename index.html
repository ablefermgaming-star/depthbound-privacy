Privacy Policy

Depthbound: The Forgotten Cells

Developer: Ableferm Gaming

Effective date: October 22, 2026

Overview

This policy explains how information is handled when you play Depthbound: The Forgotten Cells (the "Game") on Android. The Game is developed by Ableferm Gaming and includes optional rewarded advertising.

Game progress

The Game stores gameplay information, such as character level, upgrades, equipment, dungeon progress, settings, achievements, and tombstone state, locally on your device. The current version does not require an Ableferm Gaming account and is not designed to transmit your locally saved progress to Ableferm Gaming.

Rewarded advertising and third-party information

The Game integrates the Google AdMob SDK for optional rewarded advertisements. Internal testing versions may use test ads. When advertising services are active, Google and participating advertising providers may process information such as:

  • Device and application identifiers, including the advertising ID when available and permitted;
  • IP address and approximate location inferred from IP address;
  • App and device information, including operating system and device type;
  • Ad impressions, interactions, and related usage information; and
  • Crash, diagnostic, and performance information.

Such information may be used to deliver and measure ads, prevent fraud, maintain service security and, where permitted, personalize advertisements. Rewarded ads are optional; declining to watch them does not block ordinary dungeon gameplay.

The Game includes consent handling using Google's User Messaging Platform where required. Advertising availability may depend on applicable consent and privacy choices. For Google's own practices and controls, visit Google's Privacy Policy.

Information the Game does not ask for

The Game does not ask players to provide their real name, contacts, photographs, precise GPS location, credit card number, or an Ableferm Gaming account password. Future versions may have different features; this policy will be updated if those features change the Game's data practices.

Storage, retention, and deletion

Saved game progress remains on your device until you clear the Game's app data or uninstall the Game. The Game does not offer an online Ableferm Gaming account for deleting server-side game progress. Information handled by Google or other advertising providers is subject to their own data-retention, deletion, and privacy settings. Where supported, Android lets you reset or delete the device advertising ID in system settings.

Children's privacy

The Game is not directed to children under 13. Ableferm Gaming does not knowingly request personal information directly from children under 13. If you have concerns about a child's information, use the privacy contact option below.

Data security

The Game keeps its own gameplay save data on the device. Advertising-related information is handled by Google's services according to their published security and data practices. No system can guarantee absolute security.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the Game, its advertising integrations, or legal requirements change. Updated versions will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
